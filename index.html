Born and raised in Riverside, California, I have always believed that meaningful ambition starts with deep community roots. A proud graduate of Riverside Poly High School, I developed my discipline early as a competitive cross country and track athlete, learning firsthand how daily persistence, mental endurance, and pacing lead to long-term results. Today, as an undergraduate at the University of California, Riverside pursuing a degree in Business Administration with a strong concentration in economics, I channel that same structured drive into analyzing market mechanisms, business strategy, and quantitative problem-solving. My collegiate coursework in microeconomics and managerial principles fuels my analytical curiosity, while my academic journey is anchored by a clear vision: earning my bachelor's degree and ultimately pursuing a master’s to lead impactful business ventures from the ground up.

My professional foundation reflects a hands-on work ethic and an entrepreneurial mindset across service, operations, and self-directed initiatives. Currently serving as a bakery and front-of-house team member in high-volume dining, I manage fast-paced inventory presentation, uphold exacting quality standards, and deliver seamless guest service in an environment where precision and composure under pressure are non-negotiable. Complementing this operational background, I previously launched and managed an independent residential sanitation and exterior maintenance service, handling direct sales, client scheduling, pricing strategies, and end-to-end service delivery across Riverside neighborhoods. Balancing high-volume commercial operations with independent venture management has sharpened my customer relations skills, logistical problem-solving, and appreciation for the operational discipline required to make any enterprise thrive.

Beyond the classroom and workplace, my drive is fueled by a love for outdoor exploration, travel, and hands-on mechanical projects. Whether navigating scenic coastal camping routes along the Pacific coast, logging training miles on local foothills and community trails, or working with tools to complete custom vehicle modifications, I am constantly drawn to challenges that demand focus, patience, and adaptability. I approach my personal and professional future with big dreams and an active commitment to continuous growth. Looking ahead, I aim to merge my background in practical operations, economic analysis, and community-centered leadership to build innovative, sustainable businesses that deliver genuine value, drive local economic vitality, and open new avenues for purposeful growth.
